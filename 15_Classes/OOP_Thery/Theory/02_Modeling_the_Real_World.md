### The Philosophy: Modeling the Real World

The theory of OOP is based on the idea 
that human brains don't think in functions; 
they think in things.

If I tell you to think of a "Car," 
you don't think of a list of 500 mechanical steps. 
You think of an object that has:

    1. Behaviors (Actions): Drive, Brake, Honk.
    2. Properties (State): Color, Fuel Level, Speed.


OOP allows a programmer to build a "Blueprint" (a Class) for that car. 
Once you have the blueprint, 
you can stamp out 1,000 "Cars" (the Objects), 
each with its own color and fuel level, 
but all following the same rules.
